Design notes

Reasoning

Show the work, then get out of the way

The steps name what the assistant is actually reading (August transactions, July comparison). When the answer arrives the card collapses to one line so it never competes with the insight. Tap to reopen.

Primary response

One sentence is the answer. The card is evidence.

Total, direction versus last month, and the single thing that changed, in plain text like any other reply. The card sits under it as support, not as a dashboard dropped into a bubble.

Earns a tap

Four categories, three behind a fold

The stacked bar gives proportion at a glance. Only the four biggest rows show by default; the rest are one tap away, so the message stays roughly one screen tall.

Drill-down

Tapping a category asks a question

A category row posts a follow-up turn instead of opening a modal. The drill-down lives in the conversation history and can be shared, scrolled back to, or asked again in words.

Round-Up

Only where the data makes it relevant

The Round-Up moment appears inside a drill-down with many small card purchases (dining, transport, groceries), using the real spare-change figure for that category. It never appears on the headline recap, transfers or bills. "Not now" removes it for the session.

Monthly habit

The ritual is opt-in

"Send this every month" is a follow-up chip, not a nudge. Accepting it gives a dated confirmation, so the user knows exactly when the next recap lands.
